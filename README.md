# ads505-final-project
Business Objective:
Use early outreach campaigns targeted at customers who are most likely to default on their monthly payments to decrease the number of credit card defaults.
Data Mining Goal:
Create a classification model that labels customers as defaulter or non-defaulter with a 95% accuracy rate.
Success Criteria:
Reduce the percentage of overall credit card defaults for the quarter by 1%.
Report to Head of Credit Risk & Collections Department of Taiwan Global Bank
Dataset:
https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/350/default+of+credit+card+clients
23 attributes and 30,000 instances
